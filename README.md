# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn in a telecommunications company to understand customer retention, identify the major drivers of churn, and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.

The analysis was conducted using Microsoft Excel and Power BI, with a focus on customer demographics, tenure, contract type, payment methods, and subscribed services.

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Understand the overall customer base and retention level.
- Identify customer segments with high churn rates.
- Determine whether contract type influences customer retention.
- Analyze the relationship between tenure and customer loyalty.
- Identify services associated with higher churn.
- Determine which payment methods have higher churn.
- Provide recommendations to help reduce customer churn.

## Business Questions

1. What does the customer base look like?
2. Which customer segments have the highest churn?
3. Does contract type influence retention?
4. Does tenure affect customer loyalty?
5. Which services influence churn?
6. Which payment methods have higher churn?

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains approximately 7,000 customers.
- The overall customer retention rate is approximately 73.5%.
- The overall churn rate is approximately 26.54%.
- New customers have a higher churn risk compared with customers who have been with the company for longer periods.
- Month-to-month customers show greater churn exposure than customers on longer-term contracts.
- Electronic check customers have the highest churn rate among the payment methods analyzed.
- Customer services and subscription patterns provide important indicators of churn behavior.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Improve onboarding for new customers**  
   Introduce stronger onboarding and early-stage engagement programs to reduce churn among new customers.

2. **Encourage longer-term contracts**  
   Provide incentives for customers to move from month-to-month contracts to one-year or two-year plans.

3. **Review electronic-check customers**  
   Investigate why customers using electronic checks have higher churn and encourage alternative payment methods where appropriate.

4. **Strengthen customer engagement**  
   Develop targeted retention campaigns for customers showing signs of disengagement.

5. **Use customer segmentation for retention strategies**  
   Create targeted offers and interventions based on tenure, contract type, services, and payment method.
